How It Works

Four steps from congestion to clear roads

1

Sensors Detect

Cameras and radar sensors placed on the road measure vehicle density, speed, and queue length every second.

2

AI Analyses

Our AI processes all sensor data and predicts if traffic will exceed safe limits in the next few minutes.

3

Road Lifts

When the AI gives the signal, the elevated road rises and the smart ramp lowers, opening the second lane.

4

Traffic Flows

Vehicles move onto both layers. When traffic clears, the AI lowers the elevated road back down automatically.
